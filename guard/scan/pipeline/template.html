Toyota Camry, scanned and taken apart

One phone walk-around of a Royal Motors test-drive Camry, rebuilt in 3D and split into 32 exterior parts. Pick a part to see its size and search for it.

Drag to orbit · scroll or pinch to zoom · tap a part

How it was built

  • Camera positions for all 95 views (video frames at 3 fps plus 9 photos) by Structure from Motion.
  • Every frame split into car parts by a segmentation model, then given depth by a monocular depth network, aligned to the 3D points of that view.
  • Kept only points seen in at least 3 views; each point takes the part most views agree on. Left and right come from the car's own axes.

Size check

Length is used to set the scale, so only width and height are a real test. Width includes the mirrors in the scan.

Use it in other apps

The scan exports as camry_parts.glb (one named node per part) and parts.json (names, sides, sizes, centres, search queries). Any 3D viewer, AR app, parts shop or insurance tool can read them.

Exterior parts only: hidden parts are not visible to a camera and would come from the official parts catalogue by VIN. The number plate is removed from the scan, and only pixels on the car are used, so people around it are not in the model. Models used: car-parts segmentation by mitbersh/AutoInspect (YOLO26-s, Hugging Face; licence not stated by the author, used here for a demo), Depth Anything V2 Small (Apache-2.0), COLMAP via pycolmap (BSD). Part searches go to public search pages; part numbers are not verified.
