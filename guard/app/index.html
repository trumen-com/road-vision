    Mounting the phone in a car
    1. Mount the phone on the windscreen behind the mirror, camera facing forward, landscape if possible.
    2. Open this page, tap Use my camera and allow camera and location.
    3. Tap Adjust lane and drag the yellow point to where your lane meets the horizon. It is remembered.
    4. Keep the screen on and the sound up. Warnings are silenced below 8 km/h, so traffic queues stay quiet.
