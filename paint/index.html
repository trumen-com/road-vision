    How it works
    
    
    
      
    One photo per joint
    Both panels in one shot share the same light, so their colours can be compared fairly. Across two photos they could not be.
    
      
    Paint at equal brightness
    Two panels face the light at different angles. We take the reflection-free part of each side and compare colour at the same brightness (CIEDE2000 colour difference).
    
      
    Texture and gloss
    Repaints are often rougher (orange peel, sanding marks) and reflect less sharply than the factory clear coat. Both are measured next to the colour.
    
      
    The odd one out
    A panel is called repainted when it differs from every neighbour it was compared with. Plastic bumpers may differ a little from the metal at the factory, so their joints need a bigger difference.
    
    
    
    
    Score: below  same paint · up to  small difference · above that, different paint. Screening only: confirm flagged panels with a paint thickness gauge before pricing.
